Typography

Rajdhani — headers, archetype names, labels, all caps

Inter — body copy, descriptions, questions. Free on Google Fonts, pairs with Rajdhani's condensed technical feel without competing with it.

Screen mock — question

QUESTION 3 OF 7
A setback happens. You:
Get back up immediately, no time to think
Analyze what went wrong first
Get mad, then move on fast
Laugh it off, wasn't that deep

Angled corner cut (clip-path) on panels and options instead of rounded corners, this is the single biggest signature of the tactical-UI look. Left border on options fills with the accent color on hover/select.
